Unbroken: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Unbroken is a streak, goal and habit counter for iPhone and Apple Watch. This policy covers the Unbroken app.

What the app collects

Nothing. Unbroken does not collect, transmit or share any personal data.

Where your data is stored

The streaks, goals and habits you create, including their names, start and end times, check-ins and notes, are stored only on your device. If you use the Apple Watch app, your current streaks are sent directly from your iPhone to your own Apple Watch so the watch can show them. They are not sent anywhere else.

If you back up your device with iCloud or a computer, your Unbroken data may be included in that backup, under the terms you have with Apple.

Microphone and voice input

If you tap the microphone button, Unbroken listens so that you can create a streak, goal or habit by saying it. Your speech is turned into text on your device by Apple's on-device speech recognition. The audio and the text are not stored and are not sent to us or to anyone else. The microphone is only used while the voice screen is open, and you can use the app without ever allowing it.

Notifications

If you turn on a daily notification for a streak or goal, it is scheduled on your device. No notification is sent from a server.

Accounts, analytics and advertising

Unbroken has no accounts and no sign-in. It contains no analytics, no advertising and no third-party software that tracks you. The app does not connect to the internet.

Deleting your data

You can delete any streak, goal or habit, or any entry in its history, inside the app. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device.

Children

Unbroken does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.

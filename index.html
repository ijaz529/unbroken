Unbroken: Support

Unbroken is a free app for iPhone and Apple Watch that counts your streaks, counts down to your goals and tracks your habits.

Contact

Questions, problems or ideas: ijaz529@gmail.com

Common questions

How do I add a widget?

Touch and hold an empty area of the Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and search for Unbroken. To choose what a small widget shows, touch and hold it and tap Edit Widget.

My streaks do not show on my Apple Watch

Open Unbroken on your iPhone with the watch nearby. The watch receives your streaks when the iPhone app is opened or when a streak changes.

I ended a streak by mistake

Open the streak, find the run under History, swipe it to the left and delete it. Then edit the streak and set its start time back to when it really began.

Voice input does not work

Voice input needs the Microphone and Speech Recognition permissions. You can turn them on in Settings, under Apps, Unbroken.

Where is my data?

Only on your device. Deleting the app deletes its data. See the privacy policy.
